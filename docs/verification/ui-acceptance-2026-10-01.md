## Issues found and fixed during this gate

1. Light-mode tertiary text was 3.26:1 (help text, counts, footer, inactive tab). Raised `--ink-3` to #6e6e75 (5.06:1 on white, 4.56:1 on fields) and `--ink-2` to #5c5c63.
2. Phone home overflowed 36 px horizontally with a long card name: grid columns now `minmax(min(260px,100%),1fr)`, tiles and captions `min-width: 0`.
3. Editor template chips were clipped on desktop without a scroll affordance: they now wrap.

The gate was re-run after the fixes: all checks pass.
