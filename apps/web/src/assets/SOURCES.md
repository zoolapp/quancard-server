# Web assets

All images here are original QuanCard brand artwork (the otter and the kaka mark), made for
the project and used with the owner's permission. They are re-encoded from the iOS app
(`QuanCard/Assets.xcassets`: `brand-otter`, `otter-guardian`, `otter-scene-first-card`) and the
quancard.app site assets. `public/favicon.svg` is the kaka mark; `public/icon-*.png` and
`public/apple-touch-icon.png` come from `AppIcon-Light.png`.

Not included on purpose: issuer, network and wallet logos (pending trademark review) and the
iOS demo card photos (unlicensed, internal preview only). Card faces use the original templates
and issuer colour palettes instead.
